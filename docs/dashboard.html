        How the match score is computed — and its limits
        • Required skills: extracted from the offer text by qwen3:8b running locally, then normalized (case, spelling, French/English aliases) and de-duplicated; soft skills removed.
        • Coverage = matched skills / required skills. Score = (sum of my level / 5 over matched skills) / required skills × min(1, required / 3). A skill I rate 2/5 counts less than one I rate 5/5, and an offer naming only one or two skills cannot score high.
        • Levels are my own self-assessment; a match means "the offer names a skill I list", not "I meet the offer's level".
        • Offers come from public job APIs (Arbeitnow, Jobicy, Remotive, Remote OK) and the official career-site APIs of companies hiring in France. Only sources that allow showing their jobs with a link back are published, and only title, company, link, extracted skills and score — never the offer text. Remotive and anything scraped with jobspy stay on my laptop.
        • Compensation is rarely stated in internship offers, so it is reported as a count, not charted.
